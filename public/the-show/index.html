the show

Conversations with the people behind motorsport.

161 LAPS is a new interview show about the people, stories and lives behind motorsport — not just what happens on the racetrack.

Hosted by Paul Burfitt, 161 LAPS brings together drivers, team owners, engineers, sponsors, officials, promoters, media people and others who have built their lives around the sport.

The conversations are relaxed, personal and genuine.

There’s no race recap, no press-conference script and no requirement to arrive with something to promote.

Instead, we talk about the experiences people don’t always get asked about.

  • How did you get started?
  • What nearly stopped you?
  • What did motorsport teach you?
  • What happened when things didn’t go to plan?
  • Who helped you along the way?
  • What happens away from the track — or when the racing eventually stops?

And sometimes the simplest question is the most interesting:

What have you got to lose?

The Format

A relaxed one-on-one video conversation, generally recorded remotely.

Allow approximately 45–60 minutes.

There’s no preparation required and no list of rehearsed answers. The aim is simply to have a great conversation and see where it goes.

Episodes are published across video, podcast and social channels, with shorter moments from the conversation also used to introduce your story to a wider audience.

Who is it for?

161 LAPS is for people who love motorsport — but also for anyone interested in ambition, careers, risk, success, failure, reinvention and the stories behind interesting people.

The show begins in Australian motorsport but is being built with a global audience and guest list in mind.

Why 161 Laps?

The Bathurst 1000. Mount Panorama. 161 laps.

One of the most iconic races in motorsport. A thousand kilometres of preparation, determination, teamwork, risk and sometimes a little luck.

But here's the thing.

Everything can go right for 160 and even 161 laps, and it can all change in an instant.

That's motorsport. And in many ways, that's life.

The victories, the setbacks, the unexpected turns and the people who help you along the way.

161 LAPS is about the stories behind those moments — on and off the track.

You're Invited

I’d love you to join me for an episode.

No hard sell. No gotcha questions. No corporate script.

Just a genuine conversation about your story and the road that got you here.

What have you got to lose?

Paul Burfitt
Founder & Host — 161 LAPS
